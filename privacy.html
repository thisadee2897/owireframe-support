Privacy Policy

OWireframe stores your projects and editor settings on your device. The manual editor works offline. We do not operate a project server, advertising network or analytics service, and do not sell your data.

Optional Codex on Mac

If you connect Codex and send a prompt, your prompt, conversation and relevant project content are sent to OpenAI to provide the design assistant. Your ChatGPT account identifiers and authentication information are handled by Codex and OpenAI. OWireframe does not receive your ChatGPT password. Account credentials and conversation history used by the assistant are stored in the app's private local container. This feature requires an OpenAI account and network access. Review OpenAI's privacy policy before using it. The iPad app has no Codex feature.

Your choices

You may use the editor without connecting Codex. Disconnect or sign out to stop using the assistant. Do not include credentials or sensitive information in design prompts. To request help with removing local data or to ask a privacy question, contact thisadee3887@icloud.com.
