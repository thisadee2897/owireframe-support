Working with OWireframe

Create projects and screens, arrange rows, columns, grids, lists and scroll areas, or add components from the visual UI Library. Select a component to edit its text and appearance. Use Prototype to test forms, navigation and interactions.

Work is saved locally and automatically. Version 1.0 does not provide cross-device iCloud sync. Mac and iPad require macOS 26 or iPadOS 26 and later. Codex is optional and available on Mac only. Prototype forms use demo state; they do not authenticate against a real service.
